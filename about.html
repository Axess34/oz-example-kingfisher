Our story

Local plumbers you can trust

Kingfisher Plumbing started in 2012 with one ute and one plumber. Today we’re a team of five licensed plumbers and gasfitters, still family-run and still based in Stones Corner.

Most of our work comes from word of mouth: homeowners, property managers and local businesses who call us back year after year.

Every plumber carries ID, wears shoe covers and cleans up after the job. We give you a written price before we start, and that’s the price you pay.
